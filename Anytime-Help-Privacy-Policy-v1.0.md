# ANYTIME HELP — PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** 31 August 2026
**Effective Date:** 31 August 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how **Anytime Help** ("Anytime Help", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Anytime Help mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "**Platform**").

Anytime Help is a resident-focused platform that allows residents to raise complaints and service requests, submit supporting photographs or documents, receive updates, communicate with authorised administrators, and track the progress of their requests.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

# 3. INFORMATION WE DO NOT WANT YOU TO SUBMIT

Anytime Help does not generally require highly sensitive personal information for ordinary complaint and resident-management functions.

Unless specifically requested and legally permitted for a particular purpose, you should **not submit**:

* Aadhaar numbers
* Passport numbers
* Government identification numbers
* Bank account numbers
* Credit or debit card numbers
* Biometric information
* Health or medical information
* Information concerning caste
* Information concerning religion
* Information concerning political views
* Information concerning sexual life
* Other highly sensitive personal information

If such information is accidentally submitted, including through a photograph, document or complaint description, we may redact, restrict or delete it where reasonably practicable.

# 4. PHOTOGRAPHS, VIDEOS AND ATTACHMENTS

Anytime Help allows users to attach photographs, videos, audio recordings and documents to complaints.

When submitting an attachment, you should provide only information that is reasonably necessary to explain or resolve the issue.

Whenever possible, **photograph the problem rather than the person**.

Please avoid unnecessarily capturing:

* People's faces
* Children
* Domestic workers
* Security guards
* Vehicle registration numbers
* Private documents
* House interiors
* Personal identification documents
* Other residents' private information

If an attachment contains unnecessary personal information, we may redact or remove it where reasonably practicable.

# 6. HOW WE SHARE COMPLAINT INFORMATION

The purpose of Anytime Help is to help residents get their complaints resolved.

When you submit a complaint, it may be necessary to share relevant information with the people responsible for investigating or resolving that complaint.

For example:

### Housekeeping complaint

Information may be shared with the relevant housekeeping agency, supervisor or authorised RWA administrator.

### Security complaint

Information may be shared with the security agency, security supervisor, RWA or, where appropriate, law-enforcement authorities.

### Plumbing complaint

Information may be shared with the relevant plumber, technician, contractor or RWA administrator.

### Electrical complaint

Information may be shared with the relevant electrical contractor or technician.

### Parking complaint

Information may be shared with the RWA, parking team or security personnel.

### Waste-management complaint

Information may be shared with the relevant waste-management contractor or authority.

### Utility complaint

Information may be shared with the relevant electricity, water or other utility provider where necessary.

We aim to follow a **need-to-know principle** and share only information reasonably necessary to investigate or resolve the complaint.
